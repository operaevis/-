01 · THE SYSTEM

Mind, Body, and Metabolism inside a Curved Survival Manifold

Zero added hardware. OPVS plugs into existing humanoid telemetry, autonomous battery swap stations, and factory MES over a private permissioned append-only ledger (mTLS, Ed25519 X-Opvs-Signature, UUID v4 Idempotency-Key).

Work → Earn → Swap Loop

Every machine occupies a real-time coordinate (X, Y, Z) in phase space: mechanical work (X), operational capital balance (Y), and onboard battery energy (Z). Battery swaps reset Z and isolate net margin ΔM.

R_cycle = (ΔW, ΔM, 0) · θ = arctan(ΔM / ΔW)

Supervisor & Soft Bankruptcy

Four zones govern fleet safety: Ascending (d_S > +15 OPVS), Stasis, Conservation (< 10 OPVS or τ_0 < 8 h), and Plane Breach. Zone 3 grants emergency swap credit so factory lines never halt while rolling back failing AI policies and severing Zombie Helices.

d_S,i(t) = Y_i(t) − Y_S,i(X_i, Z_i, τ_i, T_i)

Kirchhoff Audit Closure

Inside a closed industrial perimeter, electrical substation meters verify every token debited across robot wallets. The residual ε_drift diagnoses battery degradation, BMS sensor drift, or unauthorized draw.
