ABSTRACT

Executive Abstract & What Changes in v2.0

Autonomous robots are entering factories, warehouses, homes, and streets. They consume electrical energy, accumulate mechanical wear, and produce measurable industrial value, yet they operate without economic identity, wage, incentive, or financial accountability. This whitepaper specifies OPVS (Operae Vis System), a settlement unit for machine labor pegged strictly to physical energy (1 OPVS ≡ 1 kWh), transforming machine work into a self-auditing metabolic economy. Building on the 2016 D-TEAM concept and a 2026 demonstration prototype, we define a four-layer settlement architecture that adds no hardware, anchored on the Work → Earn → Swap loop. We place every robot in a three-dimensional energy–economic phase space, generalize its solvency threshold into a curved Survival Manifold S carrying quadratic torque fatigue and Arrhenius-type thermal ageing, and introduce Survival Plane Supervisor Mode: a real-time governance and selection layer that isolates parasitic AI policies (Zombie Helices) and preserves physical factory continuity through controlled Soft Bankruptcy, while keeping safety constraints outside the economy. We close the physical-financial loop with Kirchhoff’s Law of the Machine Ledger, specify the OPVS Core API (v1), and detail a 90-day reference pilot—ten swap-capable electric humanoids on one closed industrial site—designed to publish the first audited balance sheet produced by a machine.

01

Introduction and Lineage

1.1 The Economic Blind Spot in Modern Robotics

Humanoid and autonomous mobile robots are crossing the threshold from laboratory prototypes to industrial fleets. Yet despite rapid advances in embodied perception, dexterity, and electrical actuation, deployed robots remain economically mute. A machine draws kilowatt-hours from the grid, fatigues harmonic drives and actuators under load, and executes revenue-generating tasks, yet possesses no wallet, no balance sheet, and no direct economic feedback linking its control policy to its physical survival.

This architectural gap produces two compounding inefficiencies: simulation policies suffer from proxy-metric drift when rewarded solely for raw speed, and operators deploying high-capital humanoids (six-figure units; no public list price) lack an hourly machine-level unit of account tying physical output to energy.

1.2 Historical Lineage: 2016–2026

OPVS descends from D-TEAM, a 2016 concept developed at Strate School of Design: a heterogeneous fleet of humanoid, aerial and plant-care robots rented through a dedicated token across six operational services, with human-to-robot tipping (TIP, slide 43) already specified in the 2016 protocol. In 2026 the concept is rebuilt from first principles and inverted: from a currency humans use to rent robots, to the currency robots themselves earn. A 2026 demonstration prototype already exercises the loop end to end: telemetry feed, AI task dispatcher, 500 OPVS wallet model, and M2M settlement ledger.

Economic Model and the Energy Parity Invariant

2.1 Strict Thermodynamic Parity

1 OPVS ≡ 1 kWh ≡ 3.6 × 10⁶ J(1)
1 OPVS = 1 000 000 µOPVS,   1 µOPVS = 3.6 J(2)

Metered energy converts exactly: 1 Wh = 1,000 µOPVS. Finer readings round half-to-even to 1 µOPVS, and the remainder joins ε_drift (§6). A signed 64-bit account holds up to 9.2 × 10¹² OPVS (~9,223 TWh). Currency sign: Θ (U+0398)—drawn, it is the OPERAE VIS mark. The peg fixes quantity, not price; whoever denominates machine labor in energy defines the native unit of account of the robotics economy.

2.2 Initial Endowment, Wallet Anatomy, and Net Margin

  • Operational Liquidity (Y_op): spendable balance in µOPVS; peer credit sits as tagged contra-balance L, so solvency tests use net liquidity Y = Y_op − L.
  • Maintenance Escrow (R_maint): ring-fenced capital accrued each cycle from operating time and measured wear (§4.1), released only against maintenance performed.
  • Income Statement (M): cumulative net profit or loss recorded per cycle.
ΔM_t = Wage_t − E_swap,t − ΔR_maint,t(3)

2.3–2.4 M2M Markets, Human Tipping, & Issuance Invariant

Peer credit is a liability repaid from future wages and never counts as margin (§5.3). Human micro-tips (2016 D-TEAM lineage) are capped per issuer/shift and excluded from policy replication. On the closed permissioned pilot ledger, the operator’s treasury is the sole issuer and every block enforces: ∑ minted − ∑ burned = ∑ balances.

04

The Curved Survival Manifold and Geodesic Control

Operating near 30 kg sustained or 50 kg instantaneous payload limits induces super-linear actuator fatigue and accelerated thermal ageing (Atlas battery life halves from 4 h to 2 h under heavy lifting [1]). Replacing constant wear with stress integrals warps the flat plane into curved Survival Manifold S:

Y_S(t) = (Z_cap − Z(t)) + R_0 + ∫_{t_0}^{t} [ r_0 + α ‖τ(u)‖² + β (e^{γ(T(u)−T_nom)} − 1)^+ ] du (10)

Where R_0 is the standing reserve, r_0 baseline depreciation, α‖τ(u)‖² quadratic torque fatigue, and β(e^{γ(T−T_nom)} − 1)^+ the Arrhenius thermal wall (γ = ln 2 / 10 K reproduces winding life halving per 10 K). Optimal control becomes geodesic navigation: minimizing torque-squared and thermal integrals per verified output aligns the economic optimum with minimum-effort control.

Zombie Helix Detection: across 3 consecutive swaps, if θ_i^int < 0 while θ_i^app ≥ 0, or peer debt L_i grows while θ_i^int ≤ 0, Supervisor severs inbound M2M credit and forces Soft Bankruptcy. Safety Precedence: no OPVS amount can buy a relaxation of force, speed, separation, or thermal limits; the economy never commands motion.
